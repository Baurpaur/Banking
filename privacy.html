Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. What this application is

"Personal Budget Tracker" is a private, non-commercial software tool developed and used by one person, Maximilian Baur, to analyse his own bank accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Data controller

Maximilian Baur, Germany. Contact for data protection matters: baurmax2002@gmail.com

3. Data processed

With his own explicit consent given at his bank, the application reads account balances and transactions (amounts, dates, counterparties, payment references) of the developer's own accounts via the Enable Banking open banking API. No data of other persons is collected on purpose; counterparty names appearing in transactions are processed only as part of the developer's own statements.

4. Purpose and legal basis

The data is used solely for personal budgeting and expense categorisation (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, consent of the account holder; the household exemption of Art. 2(2)(c) GDPR also applies).

5. Storage and sharing

Data is stored locally on the developer's own devices. It is not sold, published, used for advertising or analytics, or shared with third parties. The only external party involved is Enable Banking Oy, which acts as the licensed account information service provider between the bank and the application; see Enable Banking's privacy information.

6. Retention and withdrawal of consent

Data is kept as long as the developer uses the tool and can be deleted at any time by deleting the local database. Bank access consent expires automatically (at most 180 days) and can be revoked at any time in the bank's online banking or by deleting the session.

7. Rights

You may contact the address above regarding access, correction or deletion of data. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
